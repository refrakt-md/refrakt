# v0.43.0 — Section is the composition base

A composition that wants a titled region with media places `{% section %}`, not
`{% card %}`. `section` gains a body role, a header grammar that no longer reads prose as a
header, the media split, `cover` and a media kind. The composed storytelling runes move onto
it, and two new primitives arrive: `{% actions %}` for rows of buttons and a block-level
`{% link %}`. `card` renders exactly as it does today ({% ref "ADR-041" /%},
{% ref "ADR-046" /%}, {% ref "SPEC-160" /%}).

## Why

**Every composition with media places a drawn object.** `card` is the only primitive with a
media split, so the composed `character`, `realm` and `faction` render a surface they do not
want. The composed `character` also lost its round portrait, because a composition cannot set
`data-media`.

**`section` reads prose as a header.** A leading paragraph becomes an eyebrow even when no
heading follows. A composition that places an author's prose in a `section` gets it re-roled
with no warning, and so does a standalone region with media. Every page-section rune is
expected to become a composition over `section`, so its grammar is decided now, before the
first of them moves.

**Buttons and whole-region links are conventions, not primitives.** `hero` and `cta` share
button CSS by list position. Only `card` and `bento-cell` can be links, through an overlay that
keyboard and screen-reader users cannot reach.

## What lands

Eight items:

- **Cleanup first:** {% ref "WORK-650" /%} removes the inert `property: 'contentSection'`
  ({% ref "SPEC-133" /%}). The `property` field stays, deprecated and ignored, for one release.
- **`section`:**
  - {% ref "WORK-651" /%}: the body role;
  - {% ref "WORK-659" /%}: the header grammar anchored to the headline, a `header` attribute
    (`auto | headline | none`), and a warning when slot content fills a header role it is not
    named for;
  - {% ref "WORK-652" /%}: the split, `cover` and `media`.
- **Storytelling:** {% ref "WORK-653" /%} moves `character`, `realm` and `faction` to
  `section`.
- **Primitives:**
  - {% ref "WORK-654" /%}: `actions`, used by `hero` and `cta`, with SPEC-169 D1's composed
    `hero` fixture;
  - {% ref "WORK-655" /%}: `link` around blocks, choosing wrap or stretch in the identity
    transform, with `card` and `bento-cell` `href` as shorthand for it.
- **Anatomy:** {% ref "WORK-656" /%} makes the eyebrow a section role and moves the
  linked-eyebrow pill, copied in nine runes in both skeleton and Lumina, into the sections
  dimension.

## Sequencing inside the milestone

- WORK-651 → WORK-659 → WORK-652 → WORK-653, with WORK-650 before WORK-653.
- WORK-654 after WORK-652 and WORK-659 (v0.42.0's {% ref "WORK-646" /%} is done).
- WORK-656 after WORK-652, so `section`'s header anatomy is final.
- WORK-655 is independent.

**Merge one at a time.** Six of the eight items regenerate both contract copies, and WORK-654
and WORK-656 both edit `hero.css` and `cta.css` in skeleton and Lumina. Work can run in
parallel, but each PR is rebased onto the previous merge and its contracts regenerated before
it merges, rather than resolving conflicts in `structures.json` by hand.

## Deliberately not here

- `hero` and `cta` as compositions (SPEC-160 D7). They wait on SPEC-145 D14's chrome carrier,
  and on ADR-046's open question about a heading used as an eyebrow.
- `card` as a composition (SPEC-160 D3). It waits on {% ref "SPEC-159" /%}'s `materiality`.
- `recipe` and `howto` over `section` (SPEC-160 D5). The change of base is recorded, but their
  migration is its own work.
- The rest of {% ref "SPEC-133" /%}: the node moves, `data-field` limited to consumed metas, and
  the engine's row-key casing.
- A diagnostic for an author's `---` inside a slot that is placed in a host that splits on
  delimiters. Splitting slot content is relied on (SPEC-169 D1), so this is a warning to design,
  not a rule to change.
