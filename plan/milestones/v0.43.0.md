# v0.43.0 — Section is the composition base

A composition that wants a titled region with media places `{% section %}`, not
`{% card %}`. `section` gains a body role, the media split, `cover` and a media kind. The
composed storytelling runes move onto it, and two new primitives arrive: `{% actions %}` for
rows of buttons and a block-level `{% link %}`. `card` renders exactly as it does today
({% ref "ADR-041" /%}, {% ref "SPEC-160" /%}).

## Why

**Every composition with media places a drawn object.** `card` is the only primitive with a
media split, so the composed `character`, `realm` and `faction` render a surface they do not
want. The composed `character` also lost its round portrait, because a composition cannot set
`data-media`.

**Buttons and whole-region links are conventions, not primitives.** `hero` and `cta` share
button CSS by list position. Only `card` and `bento-cell` can be links, through an overlay that
keyboard and screen-reader users cannot reach.

## What lands

Seven items:

- **Cleanup first:** {% ref "WORK-650" /%} removes the inert `property: 'contentSection'`
  ({% ref "SPEC-133" /%}).
- **`section`:** {% ref "WORK-651" /%} adds the body role, and {% ref "WORK-652" /%} adds the
  split, `cover` and `media`.
- **Storytelling:** {% ref "WORK-653" /%} moves `character`, `realm` and `faction` to
  `section`.
- **Primitives:**
  - {% ref "WORK-654" /%}: `actions`, used by `hero` and `cta`, with SPEC-169 D1's composed
    `hero` fixture;
  - {% ref "WORK-655" /%}: `link` around blocks, with `card` and `bento-cell` `href` as
    shorthand for it.
- **Anatomy:** {% ref "WORK-656" /%} makes the eyebrow a section role and moves the linked-eyebrow
  pill, copied in nine runes in both skeleton and Lumina, into the sections dimension.
