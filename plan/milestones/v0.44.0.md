# v0.44.0 — Hero, cta and testimonial compose

The first marketing runes become compositions over `section`, beside the plugin runes they will
replace. Before they can, a composed rune has to put its universal attributes somewhere other
than its empty root, and state its chrome defaults ({% ref "SPEC-145" /%} D31–D34,
{% ref "SPEC-160" /%} D7, {% ref "SPEC-161" /%} D1).

## Why

**A composed rune's universal attributes land on a wrapper no theme designed.**
- `elevation="raised"` on a composition around a `section` draws a second complete surface
  around it.
- `prominence` is dropped with a warning.
- `width` is the only one that is right there.

`hero` and `cta` both depend on `prominence`, `elevation` and `width`.

**A composition cannot state a default.** A native `hero` is full width, flush and large unless
the author says otherwise, through its config. A composition has no config to say it in, and a
template cannot write on its own root.

**`section` cannot host `hero`'s media.** `hero` lets a media guest bleed past its zone, through
a config field that cannot vary between compositions placing the same `section`.

## Replacing the plugin runes is a separate step

Each composition is built beside the plugin rune and compared with it, as
{% ref "SPEC-147" /%} D2 set: rendered output for all three, JSON-LD against the SEO baseline for
`testimonial`, every difference explained. The comparison is reviewed on each rune's reference
page on the staging site, and each rune goes live in its own work item once
{% ref "SPEC-181" /%}'s four rollout gates are met (D5). The plugin runes stay. Swapping them is
decided once the comparisons are reviewed, and is not in this milestone.
