- **`pullquote` gains `variant="plain"`**: the quote set as the content of what holds it rather than as a feature. Italic at the surrounding size, with no glyph, rule or indent, and never floated, whatever its `align`. A testimonial's card is the case it is for.
- **A `start` or `end` split with no media zone lays out as one column.** A rune with `media-position` and nothing in its media zone used to leave its content in half the row.
