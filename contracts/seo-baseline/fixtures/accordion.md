## What is refrakt.md?

A content framework built on Markdoc that extends Markdown with semantic runes.

## How do runes work?

Runes are Markdoc tags that wrap ordinary Markdown.

## Do I need to learn a new syntax?

No. Runes use standard Markdoc tag syntax.
